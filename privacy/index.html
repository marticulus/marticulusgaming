Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Golf Card Game by Marticulus Gaming (the “App”). It explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

  • Golf Card Game does not require users to create an account.
  • Marticulus Gaming does not directly collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, or precise location through the game.

Advertising

  • The game uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements.
  • Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, approximate location, and other data necessary to provide, measure, personalize, and prevent fraud in advertising.
  • Users may be shown privacy or consent choices where legally required.

In-App Purchases

  • Purchases, including card backs and Remove Ads, are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • Marticulus Gaming does not receive or store users’ payment-card details.

Remove Ads

  • Purchasing Remove Ads disables interstitial advertisements in the game.
  • Purchase ownership may be restored through Apple’s StoreKit system.

Children’s Privacy

  • Marticulus Gaming does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
  • Advertising and privacy settings are handled through the applicable Apple and Google systems.

Data Retention and Security

  • Marticulus Gaming retains only information reasonably necessary to operate, support, and improve the app.
  • Third-party services process information under their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit
  • Google AdMob

Changes to This Policy

Marticulus Gaming may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will remain published on this page.
